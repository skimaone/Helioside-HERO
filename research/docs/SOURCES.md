# Primary and secondary sources (verified 2026-10-23)

1. Dave Hill Designs, **Titan compressor limiter manual v1.1** (2012), pp. 4–9. https://www.akzent-audio.de/media/unknown/21/cd/dc/titan_manual_ver_1-7872205.pdf
   - Architecture, feedback controls, 16-position settings, 6% wet on Direct, Dynamic Color, Fat/Air 12dB GR reference graph, specifications. Primary.
2. Hugh Robjohns, **Dave Hill Designs Titan**, Sound on Sound, Jan 2013. https://www.soundonsound.com/reviews/dave-hill-designs-titan
   - Third-harmonic subjective observations, analog/class-A, feedback, output range, 870kHz sidechain, parallel, transient character. Independent review.
3. **Interview series (5) — Dave Hill**, Variety of Sound, June 18 2013. https://varietyofsound.wordpress.com/2013/06/18/compressor-aficionados-5-dave-hill/
   - Designer confirms 870kHz sidechain, distinguishes PWM gain element from RMS detector; explains control vs audio path.
4. **Dave Hill Designs Titan**, Amazona, 2019. https://www.amazona.de/test-dave-hill-designs-titan-kompressor/
   - Independent review and candidate audio examples. Audio was NOT downloaded or analyzed for this package.
5. **Dave Hill Designs Titan**, Vintage King listing. https://vintageking.com/products/dave-hill-designs-titan
   - Vendor paraphrase of control descriptions; not independent technical confirmation.

No unpublished schematic, component list, impulse/step capture, native multitrack measurement set, or manufacturer transfer-curve tables were recovered or analyzed. Do not present conjectural semiconductor types or detailed component values as facts.
